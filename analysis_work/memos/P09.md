# E9 — Amina (F, camp; table age 50, work status NA; K6 = 16 in table)
DEMOGRAPHIC CONTRADICTION (work): she describes current paid work — daily shift 10:00–15:00 (C21) — continuously in NGO roles since 2016 and with Save the Children ("Coaching for Life"/Arsenal) "from 2019 to this day" (C47); "you're working now" is confirmed by the interviewer (C50, C60–C61). The table records NA. Flag.
K6 DISCREPANCY: answers in the transcript: sometimes / never / sometimes / never / never / never (C6–C16). This gives 4 on the standard 0–4 scoring or 10 on 1–4 scoring; the recorded total of 16 cannot be reproduced from the transcript. Flag for researcher; K6 used descriptively only.
Methodological note: E9 was transcribed in long turns (88 units), several mixing interviewer and participant speech; the source segmentation was kept and turns were multi-coded; speaker turns are marked in corrected translations.
Narrative arc: Commercial-institute graduate; in Syria a substitute primary teacher briefly before "committing to the home"; family refused bank/post jobs; husband reserved (C24–C25). Work "builds identity… one must keep working to feel life needs you" (C26). In Jordan, after separating from her husband she "stood at a crossroads" and became the provider (C28–C37). A clothing shop on credit failed because customers bought on credit (C45). NGO career from 2016: protection facilitator, PSS, now children's football/life-skills coach (C47–C49) — the only participant with a stable professional trajectory in the humanitarian sector.
Dignity and autonomy: economic independence = decision-making power within the family; the unemployed/retired are "put on the margin" (C43); "I am dignified to the extent that I can do without people" (C65); exploitation of women and men when in need (C65).
Age: she has not been excluded from work, but from trainings (TOT, computer, Excel, life skills) with ceilings of 25–35 (C61–C63); factory jobs 18–33 (C53). She locates the barrier in "society's view of age" rather than age itself (C56–C59) and says age did not stop her (C54) — integrates BAR-AGE and NEG-AGE.
Productivity: more active than younger colleagues, but walks less because of work fatigue and "the anxiety of camp circumstances"; "age limits your horizons a little" (C71).
Strength: plans A, B, C; "no laziness, no retreat" (C73); children.
Future: own project/bookshop, house in Syria, volunteer PSS even unpaid — "I can't sit idle; I must work" (C75).
Generation: economically "we are the ones with money" (savings, inheritance) and guidance; socially values, anti-individualism, teaching and PSS (C77–C84).
Rights: "We are the essence of a lifetime… don't put us on the shelf" (C85) — strong closing quote (previous translation had reversed it).
Reflexive note: C18 shows she remembers a previous conversation with the researcher ("I told you last time"), indicating prior acquaintance — relevant to positionality/insider status. She is also a humanitarian worker, which may shape her programme-oriented vocabulary.
